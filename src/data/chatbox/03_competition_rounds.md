# Competition Structure — The 3 Rounds

The AMSIO Global Olympiad consists of **3 rounds**. Students may enter at Round 1 (State Qualifier) and progress based on performance.

---

## Overview

| Round | Official Name | Who | Organised By |
|-------|--------------|-----|-------------|
| Round 1 | State Qualifier | Open to all eligible students | National Partner |
| Round 2 | National Finals | Top performers from Round 1 | National Partner |
| Round 3 | Global Round | Top performers from Round 2, nominated by National Partners | AMSIO International |

---

## Round 1 — State Qualifier

**Who can join:** All eligible students (Grade 1–12 for Math/Science/Languages; Grade 3–12+ for CI) who register through their school or independently.

**Format:**
- Paper-based or online — at the National Partner's discretion
- All Subject Groups can be completed within the round window
- Scheduling is flexible, within the window set by AMSIO International

**Recognition:** Gold, Silver, and Bronze medals based on percentile performance. Certificates awarded to all participants. No physical medals at Round 1 — only certificates.

Students may register for **multiple Subject Groups** in the same round.

**Permitted items on exam day:**
- Candidate card and valid ID (visible on desk)
- Pencils (HB or 2B), eraser, sharpener — paper-based exams
- Ruler and basic drawing instruments — Science exams only
- Water in a clear, label-free bottle
- For online: laptop, charger, mouse only

**Prohibited:**
- Mobile phones, smartwatches, earbuds, any communication device
- Calculators (unless specifically authorised for a particular division)
- Notes, study sheets, textbooks, reference materials of any kind
- Food and beverages other than water

---

## Round 2 — National Finals

**Who can join:** Top performers from Round 1. The National Partner determines the qualification threshold (AMSIO recommends top 60%, adjustable by each partner).

**Format:** Determined by the National Partner, consistent with AMSIO International guidelines.

**Recognition:**
- Certificates based on performance levels
- Physical medals awarded by the National Partner (Gold, Silver, Bronze)

**Qualification for Global Round:** Top students from Round 2 are nominated by their National Partner to compete at the Global Round.

---

## Round 3 — Global Round (International Finals)

**Who can join:** Top students from Round 2, nominated by their National Partner.
- Eligibility: Students from Grade 5 and above who participated in the National Finals in the same year
- Gold, Silver, and Bronze medal winners → compete in the **Premier Division**
- Certificate holders (did not win a medal) → compete in the **Scholars Division**

**Organised by:** AMSIO International.

**Location:**
- **2026–2027 inaugural cycle:** United States
- **From 2027–2028 onwards:** rotates annually among member countries

**Duration:** Approximately one week.

**Participation limits:**
- Maximum 8 students per subject / per division / per level per country
- Each student may register for a maximum of 2 subjects at one Global Round

**Programme:**
- Opening Ceremony
- Competition Days (all subjects)
- Cultural Exchange activities
- STEM Workshops
- City Tour
- Closing Ceremony and Award Ceremony

**Two competition divisions at the Global Round:**

| Division | Who qualifies |
|---------|--------------|
| Premier Division | Gold, Silver, or Bronze medal winners at National Finals |
| Scholars Division | Certificate of Participation holders at National Finals |

**Enhanced exam format at Global Round:**

| Subject | State Qualifier & National Finals | Global Round additions |
|---------|----------------------------------|----------------------|
| Mathematics | 100% MCQ | Higher grades add written component |
| Science | 100% MCQ | 100% MCQ (no written component) |
| Language | 100% MCQ | Higher grades add writing component |
| CI (Level 1) | 100% MCQ | 100% MCQ |
| CI (Level 2 & 3) | MCQ + Practical | MCQ + Practical (CS+AI or Creative Tech) |

## Registration Methods

**Institutional registration:** Through National Representatives or AMSIO Education Partners. Schools compile lists and submit them to the National Organising Committee.

**Individual registration:** Students register online via the AMSIO system at: **amsio.org**

---

## Preparation Resources

AMSIO does not require or endorse any specific coaching programme.

Resources provided by AMSIO:
- **Examination Specifications** — describes topics, question types, and difficulty levels
- **Sample Questions** — available on amsio.org for each Subject Group and Grade Level
- **Past Papers** — available from the second competition cycle onward
- **Preparation Tips** — published on amsio.org and AMSIO social media channels

A student who has genuinely understood their school curriculum — and can think flexibly — is well prepared for AMSIO without any specialised coaching.
