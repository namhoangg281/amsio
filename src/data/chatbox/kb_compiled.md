# About AMSIO International

## What is AMSIO?

AMSIO International (Alliance for International Mathematics, Science and Computational Intelligence Olympiad) is a global academic competition organisation headquartered in the United States, bringing together students from across the world to compete in Mathematics, Science, Languages, and Computational Intelligence.

AMSIO is not merely an examination — it is a movement to redefine how academic potential is measured. In an era of rapid technological change, AMSIO believes that the ability to think, reason, and solve real-world problems matters far more than the ability to memorise facts.

**Founding philosophy:** *Empowering Global Education Leaders.*

Every examination, every question, and every award at AMSIO is designed around this principle.

---

## Mission, Vision & Values

**Mission:** To partner with educational institutions and stakeholders worldwide in cultivating globally competent learners through inclusive, rigorous, and accessible academic competitions that foster intellectual growth, critical thinking, creativity, and the development of well-rounded capabilities.

**Vision:** To establish a globally respected academic assessment ecosystem that advances educational equity, strengthens academic excellence, and supports the holistic development of learners across diverse educational systems.

**Core Values:**
- **Excellence** — constantly improve professional quality, organisational processes, and the student experience to create reliable academic standards on an international scale
- **Integrity** — all AMSIO activities are built on honesty, fairness, and transparency, ensuring every student is evaluated based on their true ability
- **Innovation** — AMSIO encourages innovative thinking and the ability to apply knowledge to practice, helping students prepare for future challenges
- **Global Collaboration** — sustainable educational achievements are built through cooperation between schools, educational organisations, and the international community
- **Educational Impact** — AMSIO's ultimate goal is the positive impact brought to students, teachers, and the educational community — not just competition results

# AMSIO Subject Groups

AMSIO examinations are organised into **4 Subject Groups**. Students may register for one or more Subject Groups in the same round.

---

## Subject Groups Overview

| Subject Group | Grade Levels | Levels | Focus |
|--------------|-------------|--------|-------|
| Mathematics | Grade 1 – Grade 12 | 12 (one per grade) | Logical reasoning, problem-solving, proof, and application |
| Science | Grade 1 – Grade 12 | 6 (paired grades) | Scientific thinking, data analysis, experimental design |
| Language Olympiad | Grade 1 – Grade 12 | 6 (paired grades) | Reading, grammar, vocabulary, critical thinking |
| Computational Intelligence (CI) | Grade 3 – Grade 12+ | 3 | Algorithmic thinking, AI application, computer science, creative tech |

**Available languages:** English, Chinese, French, Japanese, and other international languages (depending on the National Partner in your country).

---

## Grade Levels

**Mathematics** has 12 levels (one per grade, Grade 1–12), because mathematical skills are highly cumulative and differ significantly between adjacent grades.

**Science** and **Language** subjects use 6 levels with paired grades:

| Level | Grade Band |
|-------|-----------|
| 1 | Grade 1–2 |
| 2 | Grade 3–4 |
| 3 | Grade 5–6 |
| 4 | Grade 7–8 |
| 5 | Grade 9–10 |
| 6 | Grade 11–12 |

**Computational Intelligence** has 3 levels:

| Level | Grade Band |
|-------|-----------|
| Level 1 | Grade 3–5 |
| Level 2 | Grade 6–9 |
| Level 3 | Grade 10–12+ |

---

## Examination Format & Duration

| Subject | Questions | Duration | Format |
|---------|-----------|----------|--------|
| Mathematics (Grade 1–5) | 25 questions | 60 minutes | MCQ |
| Mathematics (Grade 6–12) | 30 questions | 90 minutes | MCQ |
| Science (all levels) | 25 questions | 60 minutes | MCQ |
| Language (Grade 1–8) | 50 questions | 60 minutes | MCQ |
| Language (Grade 9–12) | 60 questions | 90 minutes | MCQ |
| CI Level 1 (Grade 3–5) | 30 questions | 60 min theory | MCQ only |
| CI Level 2 (Grade 6–9) | 30 questions | 90 min theory + 120 min practice | MCQ + Practical |
| CI Level 3 (Grade 10–12+) | 30 questions | 90 min theory + 120 min practice | MCQ + Practical |

All durations are fixed and may not be altered by any National Partner.

---

## Mathematics

Assesses logical reasoning, problem-solving, and mathematical application — not rote formula memorisation.

**Content areas:**
- Number Sense, Algebra, Geometry, Measurement, Data Analysis, Mathematical Reasoning

**Scoring:** +1 for correct, 0 for incorrect or unanswered. No negative marking.

---

## Science

Covers Biology, Physics, Chemistry, Earth & Space Science in an **integrated format**. Emphasises scientific thinking and real-world application over isolated factual recall.

Papers include visual elements (diagrams, graphs, photographs) requiring analytical interpretation.

---

## Language Olympiad

Available in: **English, Chinese, French, Japanese**, and other international languages (subject to National Partner).

Aligned with international language frameworks (Cambridge English / HSK for Chinese / DELF for French / JLPT for Japanese depending on level).

**Assessed competencies:** Reading, Vocabulary, Grammar, Critical Reading, Inference Skills.

---

## Computational Intelligence (CI)

The most unique subject in AMSIO. Assesses digital literacy, computational thinking, and the ability to use modern technology to solve real-world problems.

**Theory MCQ (all 3 levels):**
- Level 1 (Grade 3–5): basic computer literacy, introductory logic, sequencing, visual block-coding
- Level 2 (Grade 6–9): sorting/searching concepts, flowcharts, Boolean logic, pseudocode reading, algorithms
- Level 3 (Grade 10–12+): advanced algorithms, data structures, machine learning concepts, ethical AI

**Practical component (Level 2 and Level 3 only):**

Students choose one of two tracks:

**Track 1 — Computer Science + AI (CS+AI)**  
Solve algorithmic/data-structure problems and work with pre-trained AI models on provided datasets. Free choice of programming language.

**Track 2 — Creative Tech**  
Build a functional application using no-code or low-code tools (e.g., Bubble.io, Streamlit, GPT API). Judged on functionality, design, and creativity. Submission: live demo link + video.

| Level | MCQ Weight | Practical Weight |
|-------|-----------|----------------|
| Level 1 (Grade 3–5) | 100% | — (no practical) |
| Level 2 (Grade 6–9) | 50% | 50% |
| Level 3 (Grade 10–12+) | 30% | 70% |

---

## Cross-Level Registration (CI only)

Students may register for a **higher** Grade Level in Computational Intelligence only.

- May register for any higher level (no cap on advancement)
- May NOT register for a lower level than their actual grade
- Awards and certificates reflect the registered level, with a special cross-level notation
- **Not available** for Mathematics, Science, or Languages

Example: a Grade 7 student can register for the Level 3 (Grade 10–12+) CI examination.

---

## Design Philosophy

Every AMSIO paper is built on five design pillars:
1. **Reasoning over recall** — at least 50% of marks require multi-step reasoning
2. **Real-world anchoring** — authentic contexts: scientific data, real maps, historical scenarios
3. **Accessibility** — no culturally specific knowledge unavailable to a global cohort
4. **Differentiation** — deliberate difficulty gradient that distinguishes strong, average, and exceptional performers
5. **Fairness** — every item reviewed for linguistic, cultural, and gender bias

AMSIO does not require or endorse any specific preparation programme. A student who has engaged meaningfully with their school curriculum is well prepared.

# Competition Structure — The 3 Rounds

The AMSIO Global Olympiad consists of **3 rounds**. Students may enter at Round 1 (State Qualifier) and progress based on performance.

---

## Overview

| Round | Official Name | Who | Organised By |
|-------|--------------|-----|-------------|
| Round 1 | State Qualifier | Open to all eligible students | National Partner |
| Round 2 | National Finals | Top performers from Round 1 | National Partner |
| Round 3 | Global Round | Top performers from Round 2, nominated by National Partners | AMSIO International |

---

| December 2026 | State Qualifier takes place |

---

## Round 1 — State Qualifier

**Who can join:** All eligible students (Grade 1–12 for Math/Science/Languages; Grade 3–12+ for CI) who register through their school or independently.

**Format:**
- Paper-based or online — at the National Partner's discretion
- All Subject Groups can be completed within the round window
- Scheduling is flexible, within the window set by AMSIO International

**Recognition:** Gold, Silver, and Bronze medals based on percentile performance. Certificates awarded to all participants. No physical medals at Round 1 — only certificates.

Students may register for **multiple Subject Groups** in the same round.

**Permitted items on exam day:**
- Candidate card and valid ID (visible on desk)
- Pencils (HB or 2B), eraser, sharpener — paper-based exams
- Ruler and basic drawing instruments — Science exams only
- Water in a clear, label-free bottle
- For online: laptop, charger, mouse only

**Prohibited:**
- Mobile phones, smartwatches, earbuds, any communication device
- Calculators (unless specifically authorised for a particular division)
- Notes, study sheets, textbooks, reference materials of any kind
- Food and beverages other than water

---

## Round 2 — National Finals

**Who can join:** Top performers from Round 1. The National Partner determines the qualification threshold (AMSIO recommends top 60%, adjustable by each partner).

**Format:** Determined by the National Partner, consistent with AMSIO International guidelines.

**Recognition:**
- Certificates based on performance levels
- Physical medals awarded by the National Partner (Gold, Silver, Bronze)

**Qualification for Global Round:** Top students from Round 2 are nominated by their National Partner to compete at the Global Round.

---

## Round 3 — Global Round (International Finals)

**Who can join:** Top students from Round 2, nominated by their National Partner.
- Eligibility: Students from Grade 5 and above who participated in the National Finals in the same year
- Gold, Silver, and Bronze medal winners → compete in the **Premier Division**
- Certificate holders (did not win a medal) → compete in the **Scholars Division**

**Organised by:** AMSIO International.

**Location:**
- **2026–2027 inaugural cycle:** United States
- **From 2027–2028 onwards:** rotates annually among member countries

**Duration:** Approximately one week.

**Participation limits:**
- Maximum 8 students per subject / per division / per level per country
- Each student may register for a maximum of 2 subjects at one Global Round

**Programme:**
- Opening Ceremony
- Competition Days (all subjects)
- Cultural Exchange activities
- STEM Workshops
- City Tour
- Closing Ceremony and Award Ceremony

**Two competition divisions at the Global Round:**

| Division | Who qualifies |
|---------|--------------|
| Premier Division | Gold, Silver, or Bronze medal winners at National Finals |
| Scholars Division | Certificate of Participation holders at National Finals |

**Enhanced exam format at Global Round:**

| Subject | State Qualifier & National Finals | Global Round additions |
|---------|----------------------------------|----------------------|
| Mathematics | 100% MCQ | Higher grades add written component |
| Science | 100% MCQ | 100% MCQ (no written component) |
| Language | 100% MCQ | Higher grades add writing component |
| CI (Level 1) | 100% MCQ | 100% MCQ |
| CI (Level 2 & 3) | MCQ + Practical | MCQ + Practical (CS+AI or Creative Tech) |

# Scoring, Achievement Levels & Awards

---

## Scoring (All Rounds)

**MCQ scoring (all subjects):**
- Correct answer: **+1 point**
- Incorrect answer: **0 points** (no negative marking)
- Unanswered question: **0 points**

No negative marking — students should attempt every question, there is no penalty for guessing.

**CI Practical scoring (Level 2 and Level 3 only):**
Rubric-based scoring specific to each track (CS+AI or Creative Tech). Scores combined with MCQ according to division weightings.

---

## Round 1 — State Qualifier Awards

Awards based on performance percentile **within the same Subject Group and Grade Level within each country**.

| Award | Percentile |
|-------|-----------|
| Gold | Top 10% |
| Silver | Next 20% (top 10–30%) |
| Bronze | Next 30% (top 30–60%) |
| Certificate of Participation | Remaining 40% |

**Every participant receives a certificate.** There is no "fail" — every student who completes the examination is recognised. No physical medals at Round 1 — certificates only.

Awards based on performance percentile within the national cohort.

| Award | Percentile |

| Certificate of Participation | Remaining 64% |

**Round 2:** Certificates AND physical medals (provided by the National Partner).

## Special Awards (Global Round)

**AMSIO Excellence Cup**
Awarded to any Participant achieving Gold in **2 or more different Subject Groups** at the same Global Round. Recognises exceptional breadth of academic talent.

**AMSIO Champion Cup**
Awarded to any Participant achieving Gold in the **same Subject Group for 3 consecutive years**. Recognises sustained excellence and long-term dedication.

**National Team Cup**
Awarded to the top countries based on the average scores of each country's top Participants per Subject Group. Recognises the strength of national academic systems.

## Grade Level Rules

Each student competes in the Grade Level corresponding to their **current enrolled grade**.

**Exception — Computational Intelligence only:**
Students may register for a **higher** Grade Level in CI if they have advanced skills.
- May register for any higher level (no cap on advancement)
- May NOT register for a lower level than their actual grade
- Certificate and awards reflect the registered level, with a special cross-level notation

# Frequently Asked Questions

---

## About AMSIO

**Q: What does AMSIO stand for?**
A: AMSIO stands for Alliance for International Mathematics, Science and Computational Intelligence Olympiad.

**Q: Is AMSIO an international competition?**
A: Yes. AMSIO is a global academic olympiad operating across multiple countries through a network of National Partners. The competition culminates in a Global Round (International Finals) held in a different country each year.

**Q: How is AMSIO different from other Olympiads?**
A: AMSIO evaluates critical thinking and reasoning skills rather than memorisation. It integrates multilingual tracks and Computational Intelligence (AI & coding) as official subjects — uniquely endorsed by ABC Education Group and Polaris Global Academy with a presence in 20+ countries.

**Q: Who develops AMSIO?**
A: AMSIO was established in the United States. Examination content is developed and quality-assured in academic collaboration with ABC Education Group and Polaris Global Academy — institutions recognised for excellence in global education and assessment across the USA, UK, and Poland.

**Q: What is FOXY?**
A: FOXY is the official AMSIO mascot — a spirited fox in a navy uniform, symbolising intelligence, agility, and the pioneering spirit of young scholars. The AMSIO website chatbot is also named AMSIFOX after the mascot.

---

## Subjects & Eligibility

**Q: What subjects can students take?**
A: Four Subject Groups: Mathematics, Science, Language Olympiad (English, Chinese, French, Japanese, and other languages), and Computational Intelligence. Students may take one or more subjects in the same round.

**Q: What grades can participate?**
A: Math, Science, Languages: Grade 1–12. Computational Intelligence: Grade 3–12+ (includes university students up to Year 2).

**Q: Who is included in the Grade 12+ category?**
A: Grade 12+ includes Grade 12 students, gap year learners, pre-university students, and university students in their first and second year of study (Year 1 and Year 2), ensuring a consistent academic benchmark beyond secondary education.

**Q: Can a younger student take a harder grade's exam?**
A: Only in Computational Intelligence. Students may register for a higher CI Grade Level than their actual grade (called "grade skipping"). For Math, Science, and Languages, students must compete in their own grade's level.

**Q: Can I participate in multiple subjects?**
A: Yes. Students can register for one or more Subject Groups at any round — State Qualifier, National Finals, and Global Round.

**Q: Is there a minimum GPA or academic score to enter?**
A: No. AMSIO is open to all eligible students who wish to participate — there is no prior achievement requirement.

**Q: Who can participate?**
A: Students from public schools, private schools, international schools, homeschooling, and international study programs are all eligible. There are no restrictions on nationality or education system.

## Competition Format

**Q: How many rounds are there?**
A: Three — Round 1 (State Qualifier), Round 2 (National Finals), and Round 3 (Global Round / International Finals).

**Q: How do students qualify for the National Finals?**
A: Based on performance in the State Qualifier. The National Partner determines the qualification threshold (AMSIO recommends top 60%, adjustable per partner).

**Q: How do students qualify for the Global Round?**
A: By being nominated by their National Partner based on National Finals results. Only Grade 5+ students who participated in the National Finals are eligible.

**Q: Can I participate online?**
A: Round 1 (State Qualifier) and Round 2 (National Finals): depends on the National Partner's implementation — may be paper-based or online. Round 3 (Global Round): yes, an online participation option is always available for students who cannot travel.

**Q: What format are the exams?**
A: State Qualifier and National Finals are all multiple-choice (MCQ). The Global Round adds written components for Math and Languages (higher grades), and Practical components for CI Level 2 and Level 3.

**Q: How long are the exams?**
A: Mathematics: 60 min (Gr 1–5), 90 min (Gr 6–12). Science: 60 minutes. Languages: 60 min (Gr 1–8), 90 min (Gr 9–12). CI: 60–90 min theory + 120 min practical (for Level 2 & 3). Durations are fixed.

**Q: Can students use calculators?**
A: Generally no. Calculators are prohibited unless specifically authorised for a particular Grade Level. Check with your National Partner.

A: No. Incorrect answers receive 0 points. Students should attempt every question — there is no penalty for guessing.

**Q: How are awards determined?**

National Finals: Gold (top 6%), Silver (next 12%), Bronze (next 18%), Certificate of Participation (remaining 64%).

**Q: Does every student receive a certificate?**
A: Yes. Every student who completes the examination receives a Certificate of Participation. There is no "fail."

**Q: Are medals given at the State Qualifier?**
A: No — State Qualifier awards certificates only. Physical medals are awarded starting from Round 2 (National Finals).

**Q: What is the Premier Division at the Global Round?**
A: The Premier Division is for students who achieved Gold, Silver, or Bronze medals at the National Finals. It is the more competitive tier at the Global Round.

**Q: What is the Scholars Division at the Global Round?**
A: The Scholars Division is for students who received a Certificate of Participation at the National Finals (did not win a medal) but were nominated by their National Partner to compete at the Global Round.
