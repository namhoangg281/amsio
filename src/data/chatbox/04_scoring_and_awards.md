# Scoring, Achievement Levels & Awards

---

## Scoring (All Rounds)

**MCQ scoring (all subjects):**
- Correct answer: **+1 point**
- Incorrect answer: **0 points** (no negative marking)
- Unanswered question: **0 points**

No negative marking — students should attempt every question, there is no penalty for guessing.

**CI Practical scoring (Level 2 and Level 3 only):**
Rubric-based scoring specific to each track (CS+AI or Creative Tech). Scores combined with MCQ according to division weightings.

---

## Round 1 — State Qualifier Awards

Awards based on performance percentile **within the same Subject Group and Grade Level within each country**.

| Award | Percentile |
|-------|-----------|
| Gold | Top 10% |
| Silver | Next 20% (top 10–30%) |
| Bronze | Next 30% (top 30–60%) |
| Certificate of Participation | Remaining 40% |

**Every participant receives a certificate.** There is no "fail" — every student who completes the examination is recognised. No physical medals at Round 1 — certificates only.

---

## Round 2 — National Finals Awards

Awards based on performance percentile within the national cohort.

| Award | Percentile |
|-------|-----------|
| Gold Medal | Top 6% |
| Silver Medal | Next 12% (top 6–18%) |
| Bronze Medal | Next 18% (top 18–36%) |
| Certificate of Participation | Remaining 64% |

**Round 2:** Certificates AND physical medals (provided by the National Partner).

---

## Round 3 — Global Round Awards

The Global Round uses a **two-division award system**. Awards based on the **global cohort** (all countries combined).

### Premier Division (Gold, Silver, or Bronze medal holders from National Finals)

| Award | Percentile |
|-------|-----------|
| Gold Medal | Top 10% |
| Silver Medal | Next 20% |
| Bronze Medal | Next 30% |
| Certificate of Excellence | Remaining 40% |

### Scholars Division (Certificate holders from National Finals)

| Award | Percentile |
|-------|-----------|
| Gold Medal | Top 10% |
| Silver Medal | Next 20% |
| Bronze Medal | Next 30% |
| Certificate of Achievement | Remaining 40% |

---

## Special Awards (Global Round)

**AMSIO Excellence Cup**
Awarded to any Participant achieving Gold in **2 or more different Subject Groups** at the same Global Round. Recognises exceptional breadth of academic talent.

**AMSIO Champion Cup**
Awarded to any Participant achieving Gold in the **same Subject Group for 3 consecutive years**. Recognises sustained excellence and long-term dedication.

**National Team Cup**
Awarded to the top countries based on the average scores of each country's top Participants per Subject Group. Recognises the strength of national academic systems.

---

## Certificates — Key Facts

All AMSIO certificates carry:
- Participant name and registered Grade Level
- Subject Group and Award level
- Competition round and cycle year
- QR code for digital verification

Certificates are issued digitally and (for higher tiers) as printed physical documents. All certificates are verifiable online via QR code.

**Replacement certificates:** available upon request through the National Partner. A processing fee may apply.

## Benefits of Participation

**For students:**
- Internationally recognised credentials (certificates and medals) supporting university applications and scholarships
- Proof of global-standard academic achievement
- Experience competing alongside peers from multiple countries
- Development of core academic competencies: critical thinking, logical reasoning, problem-solving

**For schools:**
- External framework for assessing and recognising academic excellence
- Curriculum enrichment — AMSIO materials complement existing curricula
- Integration into global academic networks
- Marketing materials recognising school performance

**For parents:**
- Independent, internationally benchmarked assessment of a child's academic standing
- Evidence of academic performance and intellectual achievement
- Structured and secure academic environment with standardised safeguarding frameworks
