# AMSIO Subject Groups

AMSIO examinations are organised into **4 Subject Groups**. Students may register for one or more Subject Groups in the same round.

---

## Subject Groups Overview

| Subject Group | Grade Levels | Levels | Focus |
|--------------|-------------|--------|-------|
| Mathematics | Grade 1 – Grade 12 | 12 (one per grade) | Logical reasoning, problem-solving, proof, and application |
| Science | Grade 1 – Grade 12 | 6 (paired grades) | Scientific thinking, data analysis, experimental design |
| Language Olympiad | Grade 1 – Grade 12 | 6 (paired grades) | Reading, grammar, vocabulary, critical thinking |
| Computational Intelligence (CI) | Grade 3 – Grade 12+ | 3 | Algorithmic thinking, AI application, computer science, creative tech |

**Available languages:** English, Chinese, French, Japanese, and other international languages (depending on the National Partner in your country).

---

## Grade Levels

**Mathematics** has 12 levels (one per grade, Grade 1–12), because mathematical skills are highly cumulative and differ significantly between adjacent grades.

**Science** and **Language** subjects use 6 levels with paired grades:

| Level | Grade Band |
|-------|-----------|
| 1 | Grade 1–2 |
| 2 | Grade 3–4 |
| 3 | Grade 5–6 |
| 4 | Grade 7–8 |
| 5 | Grade 9–10 |
| 6 | Grade 11–12 |

**Computational Intelligence** has 3 levels:

| Level | Grade Band |
|-------|-----------|
| Level 1 | Grade 3–5 |
| Level 2 | Grade 6–9 |
| Level 3 | Grade 10–12+ |

## Examination Format & Duration

| Subject | Questions | Duration | Format |
|---------|-----------|----------|--------|
| Mathematics (Grade 1–5) | 25 questions | 60 minutes | MCQ |
| Mathematics (Grade 6–12) | 30 questions | 90 minutes | MCQ |
| Science (all levels) | 25 questions | 60 minutes | MCQ |
| Language (Grade 1–8) | 50 questions | 60 minutes | MCQ |
| Language (Grade 9–12) | 60 questions | 90 minutes | MCQ |
| CI Level 1 (Grade 3–5) | 30 questions | 60 min theory | MCQ only |
| CI Level 2 (Grade 6–9) | 30 questions | 90 min theory + 120 min practice | MCQ + Practical |
| CI Level 3 (Grade 10–12+) | 30 questions | 90 min theory + 120 min practice | MCQ + Practical |

All durations are fixed and may not be altered by any National Partner.

---

## Mathematics

Assesses logical reasoning, problem-solving, and mathematical application — not rote formula memorisation.

**Content areas:**
- Number Sense, Algebra, Geometry, Measurement, Data Analysis, Mathematical Reasoning

**Scoring:** +1 for correct, 0 for incorrect or unanswered. No negative marking.

---

## Science

Covers Biology, Physics, Chemistry, Earth & Space Science in an **integrated format**. Emphasises scientific thinking and real-world application over isolated factual recall.

Papers include visual elements (diagrams, graphs, photographs) requiring analytical interpretation.

---

## Language Olympiad

Available in: **English, Chinese, French, Japanese**, and other international languages (subject to National Partner).

Aligned with international language frameworks (Cambridge English / HSK for Chinese / DELF for French / JLPT for Japanese depending on level).

**Assessed competencies:** Reading, Vocabulary, Grammar, Critical Reading, Inference Skills.

---

## Computational Intelligence (CI)

The most unique subject in AMSIO. Assesses digital literacy, computational thinking, and the ability to use modern technology to solve real-world problems.

**Theory MCQ (all 3 levels):**
- Level 1 (Grade 3–5): basic computer literacy, introductory logic, sequencing, visual block-coding
- Level 2 (Grade 6–9): sorting/searching concepts, flowcharts, Boolean logic, pseudocode reading, algorithms
- Level 3 (Grade 10–12+): advanced algorithms, data structures, machine learning concepts, ethical AI

**Practical component (Level 2 and Level 3 only):**

Students choose one of two tracks:

**Track 1 — Computer Science + AI (CS+AI)**  
Solve algorithmic/data-structure problems and work with pre-trained AI models on provided datasets. Free choice of programming language.

**Track 2 — Creative Tech**  
Build a functional application using no-code or low-code tools (e.g., Bubble.io, Streamlit, GPT API). Judged on functionality, design, and creativity. Submission: live demo link + video.

| Level | MCQ Weight | Practical Weight |
|-------|-----------|----------------|
| Level 1 (Grade 3–5) | 100% | — (no practical) |
| Level 2 (Grade 6–9) | 50% | 50% |
| Level 3 (Grade 10–12+) | 30% | 70% |

---

## Cross-Level Registration (CI only)

Students may register for a **higher** Grade Level in Computational Intelligence only.

- May register for any higher level (no cap on advancement)
- May NOT register for a lower level than their actual grade
- Awards and certificates reflect the registered level, with a special cross-level notation
- **Not available** for Mathematics, Science, or Languages

Example: a Grade 7 student can register for the Level 3 (Grade 10–12+) CI examination.

---

## Design Philosophy

Every AMSIO paper is built on five design pillars:
1. **Reasoning over recall** — at least 50% of marks require multi-step reasoning
2. **Real-world anchoring** — authentic contexts: scientific data, real maps, historical scenarios
3. **Accessibility** — no culturally specific knowledge unavailable to a global cohort
4. **Differentiation** — deliberate difficulty gradient that distinguishes strong, average, and exceptional performers
5. **Fairness** — every item reviewed for linguistic, cultural, and gender bias

AMSIO does not require or endorse any specific preparation programme. A student who has engaged meaningfully with their school curriculum is well prepared.
