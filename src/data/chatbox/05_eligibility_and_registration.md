# Eligibility & Registration

---

## Who Can Participate?

AMSIO is open to students of **all nationalities and educational backgrounds**. There are no restrictions on nationality or education system.

**Eligible institutions and participants:**
- Public schools
- Private schools
- International schools (IB, Cambridge, AP, and other international curricula)
- Homeschooling (home-educated students may register individually using a parent-assigned school code)
- International students (students studying abroad or enrolled in cross-border academic programs)

---

## Grade Eligibility by Subject

| Subject | Eligible Grades |
|---------|----------------|
| Mathematics | Grade 1 – Grade 12 |
| Science | Grade 1 – Grade 12 |
| Language (English, Chinese, French, Japanese, etc.) | Grade 1 – Grade 12 |
| Computational Intelligence (CI) | Grade 3 – Grade 12+ |

**Grade 12+ category** includes: Grade 12 students, gap year learners, pre-university students, and university students up to **Year 2** (or equivalent). This ensures a consistent academic benchmark beyond secondary education.

There is no minimum academic score or prior achievement required to enter. AMSIO is open to all students who wish to challenge themselves.

---

## Grade Level Rules

Each student competes in the Grade Level corresponding to their **current enrolled grade**.

**Exception — Computational Intelligence only:**
Students may register for a **higher** Grade Level in CI if they have advanced skills.
- May register for any higher level (no cap on advancement)
- May NOT register for a lower level than their actual grade
- Certificate and awards reflect the registered level, with a special cross-level notation
- Example: a Grade 7 student can register for the Level 3 (Grade 10–12+) CI examination

For Mathematics, Science, and Languages, students must compete in their actual Grade Level. These subjects are strongly curriculum-dependent, so cross-level registration is not appropriate.

---

## Global Round Eligibility

To participate in the Global Round (International Finals):
- Students must be **Grade 5 or above**
- Must have participated in the National Finals in the same year
- Gold, Silver, or Bronze medal winners → compete in **Premier Division**
- Certificate holders (no medal at National Finals) → compete in **Scholars Division**
- Maximum **8 students per subject / per division / per level** per country
- Each student may register for maximum **2 subjects** at one Global Round

## How Registration Works

Registration is managed by the **National Partner** in each country.

**Two registration methods:**

1. **Institutional Registration** — Through National Representatives or AMSIO Education Partners. Schools compile student lists and submit to the National Organising Committee.

2. **Individual Registration** — Students register online via **amsio.org**. Schools registering multiple students may benefit from group coordination.

**General flow:**
1. Contact the National Partner for your country (list at amsio.org/partners)
2. National Partner provides registration details, fees, and deadlines
3. Complete registration form and pay participation fee
4. National Partner confirms registration and provides examination details (venue or online access)
5. Receive candidate card before the examination

Students may register for **one or more Subject Groups** in the same round. There is no requirement to register for all Subject Groups.

## What to Bring on Exam Day

**Permitted:**
- Candidate card and valid identification (placed visibly on the desk)
- Pencils (HB or 2B), eraser, sharpener — paper-based examinations
- Ruler and basic drawing instruments — Science examinations only
- Water in a clear, label-free bottle
- For online examinations: laptop, charger, mouse only

**Prohibited:**
- Mobile phones, smartwatches, fitness trackers, earbuds, or any communication device
- Calculators (unless specifically authorised for a particular Grade Level — confirm with your National Partner)
- Notes, study sheets, textbooks, or reference materials of any kind
- Food or beverages other than water

---

## Preparation Guidance

AMSIO does not require or endorse any coaching programme.

A student who has engaged meaningfully with their regular school curriculum is well prepared. The examinations reward reasoning and flexible thinking, not memorised facts.

**Free resources available:**
- Examination Specifications (topics, format, difficulty for each subject)
- Sample questions for each Subject Group and Grade Level — at amsio.org
- Past papers — available from the second competition cycle onward
- Preparation tips — published on amsio.org and AMSIO social media

---

## Accessibility & Accommodations

AMSIO is committed to equitable access. Participants with documented disabilities or learning needs may request accommodations through their National Partner. Typical accommodations include extended time, large-print papers, and separate examination rooms.

Requests must be submitted in advance with supporting documentation. Contact your National Partner for the specific process and timeline.
