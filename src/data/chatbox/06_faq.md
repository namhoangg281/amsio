# Frequently Asked Questions

---

## About AMSIO

**Q: What does AMSIO stand for?**
A: AMSIO stands for Alliance for International Mathematics, Science and Computational Intelligence Olympiad.

**Q: Is AMSIO an international competition?**
A: Yes. AMSIO is a global academic olympiad operating across multiple countries through a network of National Partners. The competition culminates in a Global Round (International Finals) held in a different country each year.

**Q: How is AMSIO different from other Olympiads?**
A: AMSIO evaluates critical thinking and reasoning skills rather than memorisation. It integrates multilingual tracks and Computational Intelligence (AI & coding) as official subjects — uniquely endorsed by ABC Education Group and Polaris Global Academy with a presence in 20+ countries.

**Q: Who develops AMSIO?**
A: AMSIO was established in the United States. Examination content is developed and quality-assured in academic collaboration with ABC Education Group and Polaris Global Academy — institutions recognised for excellence in global education and assessment across the USA, UK, and Poland.

**Q: What is FOXY?**
A: FOXY is the official AMSIO mascot — a spirited fox in a navy uniform, symbolising intelligence, agility, and the pioneering spirit of young scholars. The AMSIO website chatbot is also named AMSIFOX after the mascot.

## Subjects & Eligibility

**Q: What subjects can students take?**
A: Four Subject Groups: Mathematics, Science, Language Olympiad (English, Chinese, French, Japanese, and other languages), and Computational Intelligence. Students may take one or more subjects in the same round.

**Q: What grades can participate?**
A: Math, Science, Languages: Grade 1–12. Computational Intelligence: Grade 3–12+ (includes university students up to Year 2).

**Q: Who is included in the Grade 12+ category?**
A: Grade 12+ includes Grade 12 students, gap year learners, pre-university students, and university students in their first and second year of study (Year 1 and Year 2), ensuring a consistent academic benchmark beyond secondary education.

**Q: Can a younger student take a harder grade's exam?**
A: Only in Computational Intelligence. Students may register for a higher CI Grade Level than their actual grade (called "grade skipping"). For Math, Science, and Languages, students must compete in their own grade's level.

**Q: Can I participate in multiple subjects?**
A: Yes. Students can register for one or more Subject Groups at any round — State Qualifier, National Finals, and Global Round.

**Q: Is there a minimum GPA or academic score to enter?**
A: No. AMSIO is open to all eligible students who wish to participate — there is no prior achievement requirement.

**Q: Who can participate?**
A: Students from public schools, private schools, international schools, homeschooling, and international study programs are all eligible. There are no restrictions on nationality or education system.

## Competition Format

**Q: How many rounds are there?**
A: Three — Round 1 (State Qualifier), Round 2 (National Finals), and Round 3 (Global Round / International Finals).

**Q: How do students qualify for the National Finals?**
A: Based on performance in the State Qualifier. The National Partner determines the qualification threshold (AMSIO recommends top 60%, adjustable per partner).

**Q: How do students qualify for the Global Round?**
A: By being nominated by their National Partner based on National Finals results. Only Grade 5+ students who participated in the National Finals are eligible.

**Q: Can I participate online?**
A: Round 1 (State Qualifier) and Round 2 (National Finals): depends on the National Partner's implementation — may be paper-based or online. Round 3 (Global Round): yes, an online participation option is always available for students who cannot travel.

**Q: What format are the exams?**
A: State Qualifier and National Finals are all multiple-choice (MCQ). The Global Round adds written components for Math and Languages (higher grades), and Practical components for CI Level 2 and Level 3.

**Q: How long are the exams?**
A: Mathematics: 60 min (Gr 1–5), 90 min (Gr 6–12). Science: 60 minutes. Languages: 60 min (Gr 1–8), 90 min (Gr 9–12). CI: 60–90 min theory + 120 min practical (for Level 2 & 3). Durations are fixed.

**Q: Can students use calculators?**
A: Generally no. Calculators are prohibited unless specifically authorised for a particular Grade Level. Check with your National Partner.

## Awards & Scoring

**Q: Is there negative marking?**
A: No. Incorrect answers receive 0 points. Students should attempt every question — there is no penalty for guessing.

**Q: How are awards determined?**
A: By percentile ranking within the same Subject Group and Grade Level. State Qualifier: Gold (top 10%), Silver (next 20%), Bronze (next 30%), Certificate of Participation (remaining 40%). National Finals: Gold (top 6%), Silver (next 12%), Bronze (next 18%), Certificate of Participation (remaining 64%).

**Q: Does every student receive a certificate?**
A: Yes. Every student who completes the examination receives a Certificate of Participation. There is no "fail."

**Q: Are medals given at the State Qualifier?**
A: No — State Qualifier awards certificates only. Physical medals are awarded starting from Round 2 (National Finals).

**Q: What is the Premier Division at the Global Round?**
A: The Premier Division is for students who achieved Gold, Silver, or Bronze medals at the National Finals. It is the more competitive tier at the Global Round.

**Q: What is the Scholars Division at the Global Round?**
A: The Scholars Division is for students who received a Certificate of Participation at the National Finals (did not win a medal) but were nominated by their National Partner to compete at the Global Round.

## Global Round (International Finals)

**Q: Where and when is the Global Round?**
A: For the 2026–2027 inaugural cycle: **June 2027 in the United States**. From the 2027–2028 cycle onwards, the host country rotates annually among member nations.

**Q: What happens at the Global Round?**
A: Approximately one week including: Opening Ceremony, Competition Days, Cultural Exchange activities, STEM Workshops, City Tour, and Closing Ceremony with Award Ceremony.

**Q: What are the Special Awards at the Global Round?**
A: AMSIO Excellence Cup (Gold in 2+ subjects at same Global Round), AMSIO Champion Cup (Gold in same subject 3 consecutive years), National Team Cup (top-performing countries).

## Registration & Fees

**Q: How do I register for AMSIO?**
A: Contact the National Partner in your country, or visit www.amsio.org. National Partners manage all registrations, fees, and exam logistics. Full partner list at amsio.org/partners.

**Q: Can students register individually without a school?**
A: Yes, students may register individually online at amsio.org, depending on the National Partner's process.

**Q: How can my school become an AMSIO partner?**
A: Schools should contact the National Partner in their country. Organisations seeking to become a National Partner can visit **www.amsio.org/partners** to submit an Expression of Interest.

**Q: How much does it cost?**
A: Fees are set by each National Partner. Contact info@amsio.org or your country's National Partner for fee details.

## Preparation

**Q: Do students need coaching classes?**
A: No. AMSIO does not require or endorse any coaching programme. A student who understands their regular school curriculum and can think flexibly is well prepared.

**Q: Are there sample questions or past papers?**
A: Yes. Sample questions are available on amsio.org for each Subject Group and Grade Level. Past papers become available from the second competition cycle onward.

## Certificates & Verification

**Q: Are AMSIO certificates internationally recognised?**
A: Yes. AMSIO certificates are designed as internationally recognised credentials supporting university applications, scholarship applications, and academic portfolios.

**Q: How can certificates be verified?**
A: Each certificate includes a QR code for online digital verification.

**Q: Can lost certificates be replaced?**
A: Yes, through the National Partner. A processing fee may apply.
